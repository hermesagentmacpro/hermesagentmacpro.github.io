newsroom privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

newsroom is a private tool with exactly one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Google data

newsroom asks Google for one permission only: drive.file. With it, newsroom can see and change only the Google Docs that newsroom itself creates, in the owner's own Google account. It cannot see any other file in that account.

newsroom uses those Docs to write article drafts and to read the comments the owner leaves on them. It keeps copies of the drafts and comments in its own database, on a computer the owner controls.

Sharing

To revise a draft, newsroom sends the Doc's text and comments to the AI model provider the owner has configured, through OpenRouter, with the providers' training on inputs switched off. Apart from that, newsroom does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.
